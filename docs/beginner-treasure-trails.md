# Beginner Treasure Trails

Beginner clues use cache 241's `cluehelper_*` database catalogue. Nothing is fetched
from the internet when the game starts or while playing. Guided clue routes in this
plugin cover beginner clues; capacity and scroll cases use the shared ClueCapacity plugin.

## Implemented clue catalogue

The catalogue contains 26 initial clues:

| Family | Included steps |
| --- | --- |
| Anagrams (9) | Ranael; Apothecary; Archmage Sedridor; Doric; Brian; Veronica; Gertrude; Hairdresser; Fortunato |
| Cryptic NPCs (4) | Hans; Cook; Hunding; Shantay |
| Charlie (1) | Stable request for one of cooked trout, cooked pike, raw herring, raw trout, iron ore, iron dagger, leather body, leather chaps |
| Reldo (1) | Strange device, with targets at Draynor Manor mushrooms, Draynor wheat field, Ice Mountain, Lumbridge cow field, north-east of Al Kharid mine |
| Emotes (6) | Raspberry at Aris (gold ring/necklace); bow at Brugsen Bursen; cheer at Iffie (chef's hat/red cape); clap at Bob's (bronze axe/leather boots); panic at Al Kharid mine; spin at Flynn's |
| Maps (5) | Champions' Guild tree; south-east Varrock mine; south of Draynor bank; standing stones north of Falador; behind Wizards' Tower |

`Data.BeginnerTreasureTrails.js` resolves clue text, row identities, NPC IDs,
coordinates and outfits from named cache tables and columns. The old composite
map interfaces are `trail_map01/06/15/05/03`, respectively. These use their actual
cache components/models rather than a substitute text clue. Emote areas use
small radii around cache targets, with a wider area for Al Kharid mine.

## State and interaction ownership

Each scroll carries `treasure-trails:beginner` metadata: version, unique token,
revision, completed steps, trail length, clue row, and optional Charlie request,
hot/cold target and previous device reading. Existing generic item 23182 is
assigned once on first interaction. Read never consumes or rerolls a scroll.
Check steps reports completed steps. Banking, ground-item cloning, item save/load,
and death use the normal metadata-preserving item paths; no player/account is
replaced or reset. Multiple scrolls have independent state.

Trail lengths are selected at creation: 10% one step, 45% two steps, 45% three
steps. Initial clue entries are selected uniformly from the cache catalogue;
the published sources do not specify different per-clue weights.

Dialogue rewards commit only at the end of the conversation, after rechecking
the held token/revision and proximity. Interrupted or repeated callbacks cannot
grant another step or casket. Charlie accepts existing unnoted items, consumes
one, and never accepts a noted substitute. Maps require the exact plane/tile;
hot/cold digging allows the documented 9 by 9 target area. Emotes require the
actual equipped items and spawn a player-owned Uri, who must be spoken to.

The generic `onBeforeNpcInteraction` hook lets conditional content own a
matching conversation before earlier quest handlers. Unmatched interactions
fall through without setting `handled`; quest, shop and ordinary conversations
keep their existing behavior. Spade actions use the existing can-use gate so
only matching clue digs preempt other spade handlers.

## Rewards and modern capacity

The canonical reward table is generated by `osrsreboxed-db`'s reward-casket
exporter into `server/data/definitions/reward-caskets.json`: 74 entries, exact
integer weights summing to 44280. Caskets roll one to three rewards. Opening
consumes one casket, grants the actual rewards and increments the saved
completion/collection-log count. Overflow uses the existing `forceAdd` ground
reward path. Caskets cannot be opened on Entrana.

Opening prepares normal and milestone rewards before consuming anything. Every
base and credited note definition must resolve to its exact cache ID; the cache's
DEFAULT fallback is rejected. Missing milestone service, invalid reward data or
invalid saved progress leaves the casket and completion count unchanged. The
clicked stack is rechecked after preparation callbacks, and recursive callbacks
cannot open it twice.

**Unresolved fidelity gap:** the online game's anti-bot restriction
on unique rewards for accounts under ten hours is not implemented. Every local account
uses the same complete published reward table from its first casket. The Wiki
does not describe the online replacement policy, and this implementation does
not invent one or silently discard a unique reward.

Minor/major beginner scroll cases are awarded at 50/100 completions, and Explore
unlocks at 600. The shared capacity service retains those saved upgrades. Before
X Marks the Spot the receipt limit is one; afterward it starts at two and can
increase to five with consumed minor, major and Mimic cases. It counts carried
and banked clue forms across all six tiers. NPC drops and implemented skilling
sources select boxes after the quest. Box opening refuses a second active clue
in inventory, bank, grave, Death's Office or an implemented retrieval service.
An unopened full stack is preserved when no slot is available. Ground/Telegrab
capacity enforcement and its warnings remain pending; see
`server/plugins/treasuretrails/ClueCapacity.md` for the exact implemented scope.

## Verification

From `server/`, `node --test tests/beginner-treasure-trails.test.cjs` exercises every step
family against the real cache, all Charlie requests, all map and hot/cold
targets, personal Uri, interrupted/stale dialogue, metadata serialization,
rewards, Entrana, full inventory, capacity and Explore. Run after the server
build. Natural world/browser evidence is recorded with the
integration report; unit coverage alone does not establish visual parity.

`tests/beginner-casket-safety.test.cjs` adds real cache/Inventory regressions for
DEFAULT lookups, credited notes, milestone failure, stale/reentrant callbacks
and invalid saved completion counts. `tests/clue-capacity.test.cjs` covers the
shared capacity service and its real save/load path.

The isolated slot-6 world (game 43706, MCP 49806) was exercised with a dedicated
test account, using normal inventory, NPC, dialogue and emote packet paths.
Naturally assigned steps covered all six families: maps, cryptic NPCs, anagrams,
personal Uri emotes, Charlie's iron-dagger request and Reldo's device/dig route.
Four complete trails opened their real reward caskets in the final recorded
run. Earlier checks also covered Bob's clap, Ranael's anagram, and authentic
reward-screen contents (steel plateskirt and 30 body runes). A saved Sedridor
clue survived logout and a test-server restart unchanged, then completed against
his actual Archmage NPC morph. No production world or existing user character
was used. The local run record is `exports/beginner-clues/natural-route.json`
(SHA-256 `31365266cf4c0bf3caa2415fef379030dfa6756f3142af68f769408d23c958a3`).

A separate real browser account on client 3106/game 43706 rendered the authentic
anagram parchment (`Carpet Ahoy`) and the south-east Varrock mine map (group
351) from actual Read interactions. WebGPU rendering was confirmed. The browser
tab used an isolated context and was closed after verification. Client builds
pass with the repository's existing five ESLint warnings; the first build with
CI warning promotion failed on those unchanged warnings, and the normal build
completed successfully.
