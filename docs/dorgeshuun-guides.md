# Dorgeshuun tunnel guides

Kazgar, Mistag and Dartog guide players between the mine and the Lumbridge Castle tunnel. Their quick options and dialogue choices use the same crossing: the guide's message and fade out, the move three ticks later, fade in on the following tick, then the overlay closes two ticks later.

In rsprox captures 3182 and 3183, dialogue travel to the mine lands at (3309, 9613) and travel toward the castle lands at (3235, 9610). Mistag's quick return in recordings 3182 and 3187 and Kazgar's quick journey in recordings 1441, 1694, 2277, 2964, 3184, 3188, 3191, 3199 and 4263 reach the same tiles. Dartog's quick journey to the mine appears in recordings 3182 and 3186.

Recording 3182 shows Kazgar's dialogue journey to the water mill at (3242, 9648), and recording 3184 shows Mistag's at (3245, 9645). These are single recorded arrivals, so the range of possible landing tiles is not established. The quick Watermill options retain their existing arrival beside Dartog; those options were not recorded.

The water mill route opens with Death to the Dorgeshuun (Wiki, Kazgar and Mistag). The transcript offers Kazgar's and Mistag's top-level water mill dialogue options at every stage, so before the quest is complete their journey step is handled without moving the player.
