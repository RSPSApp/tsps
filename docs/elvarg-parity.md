# Elvarg quest encounter parity

The Elvarg combat provider uses the Dragon Slayer I revision 240 RSProx recording `20260825T185242-59bcb26`. Raw packets and account details remain outside the repository. Cache revision 241 supplies the NPC definitions. The [Elvarg Wiki page](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Elvarg) and [protection table](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Dragonfire/Elvarg) supply unrecorded protection combinations and the ten-percent floor Prayer drain.

`elvarg:enter` creates a private lair at the existing world coordinates, spawning owner-only base NPC 816, which the quest varp resolves to living Elvarg 8033. The first event holds aggression during the wall crossing; the second enables attacks against the owner. Other players cannot join the fight. The boss teleport menu lands outside the wall and uses the same quest entrance. It no longer leaves a global per-player process flag behind.

The owner stays in the private lair when Elvarg dies. Dragon Slayer owns the recorded death/body/head-cut sequence and progress; the provider prevents respawn and leaves the corpse area intact until departure. `elvarg:leave` destroys the private area without changing the wall's destination. Logout/disconnect clears held movement, removes the owned boss and recovers outside the wall at 2845,9636. A persisted recovery flag handles interruption by server restart. A saved Elvarg kill prevents another spawn.

Recorded combat facts:

- At 5159, 5163 and 5167, breath animation 81 and the shield message occur four ticks apart. Nearby melee uses animation 80 with the same base interval; block is 89. Both melee and breath occur in melee range, while distant attacks use breath.
- Each breath sends six fireblast projectiles 130 with launch cycles 51/56/61/66/71/75, arrival cycles 80/85/90/95/100/105, progress 200, start height 70, varying end heights 70–109 and slopes 8–23. Impact graphic 131 is sent at launch with delay 80 and height 90. Sounds are 3750 at the dragon and 156 at the target with delay 80. The implementation preserves this packet sequence and uses a three-tick delayed breath hit.
- The shield launch message is `Your shield absorbs most of the dragon fire.` Prayer is 17 at the first recorded breath, then 16 and 15 on the next two, matching subtraction of floor(current Prayer / 10). This applies even when super antifire prevents damage.
- The Wiki protection maxima are 70 unprotected, 10 with shield, 55 with prayer or antifire alone, 40 with both, 7 with shield plus either, 4 with all three, and zero with super antifire. Melee is capped at eight; quest dragon HP is 80.

From `server/`, `node --test tests/elvarg-parity.test.cjs` checks held entry, owner isolation, corpse retention, no respawn, wall exit and interrupted-session recovery, all nine protection combinations, exact projectile/graphic parameters, Prayer rounding, separate attack selections and canonical HP/death definitions. The live Dragon Slayer wall check entered a private Elvarg area, crossed from 2845 to 2847 and exposed the 80-HP boss with working dragonfire. A complete live kill with a whip and anti-dragon shield retained the private area, removed the NPC, played the head-cut sequence, awarded head item 11279 and sent wire varp 176=9 while Quest points remained zero. Canonical definition 816 is non-aggressive in the public room; aggression is enabled only for its private owned encounter.

A player under Elvarg isn't attacked: she steps off first, by the shortest open way, and attacks once clear. The Wiki's Dragon Slayer I page gives the behaviour ("it takes her a moment to move and hit you again"). Her lair's area drives this, with the combat core untouched: it pauses her and walks her off. Large NPCs otherwise fight on over an overlapped target, from #489, which ended a deadlock, so if she hasn't come clear after 8 ticks under, boxed in or stepping back and forth, she fights on the same way. The way she steps, the tick count and the bound aren't sourced.

Evidence limits: the successful capture uses an anti-dragon shield. Other protection messages and the exact damage distribution for potion combinations need corresponding captures; damage maxima follow the Wiki. The capture is consistent with a half-chance breath choice in melee range but does not prove its random weight or the maximum acquisition range. Existing half-chance and eight-tile range are retained. Random projectile appearance is reproduced within observed bounds rather than replaying one recording's random values. The player must still use the quest's normal wall entry and be eligible for Dragon Slayer I.


The quest introduction's camera is also sourced from the complete recording: look `(2856,9650)`/height150 then move `(2848,9639)`/height475 at +4, both `232/232`; look `(2866,9640)`/height150 then move `(2848,9639)`/height250 at +7, both `2/2`. All use `heightrelative=false`. The entry clears all five shakes; unlock/reset occurs at +20. The player returns to the public room at +16; camera position and targets translate with the private/public relocation, preserving the pan until release. `tests/f2p-adventure-parity.test.cjs` checks these exact shots and that reset does not occur early. This is separate from the unrecorded combat probabilities and protection combinations listed above.
