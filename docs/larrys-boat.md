# Larry's boat

The boat north-east of Rellekka gains Iceberg and Weiss options after the first trip to Weiss.
The map places a multi-loc, so the click can name its parent while the visible child supplies the
option. The same boat definition also occurs at the coast near Weiss.

## Evidence

In rsprox recordings 3282 and 3285 (revision 234, event worlds), Weiss starts with a message box
and fade, places the player on a temporary ship at +2 ticks, lands at (2849, 3968, 0) at +11, and
shows the message again at +12. The temporary ship uses the `my2arm_boatride` overlay and
`journey_number` varp. Recording 1768 (revision 227, Leagues) shows Larry speaking for the
Iceberg option; after continuing, a fade precedes the `peng_ship_journey` interface and the
landing at (2660, 3989, 1). Recordings 1767 and 1768 show three of Larry’s opening lines; their selection weights are not established.
These recordings establish the observed positive trips; they do not establish the first unlock.

The [Rellekka Wiki page](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Rellekka) describes the boat below
Weiss as a route to the dock north-east of Rellekka. The return uses the existing Rellekka landing
and immediate travel; neither its precise landing tile nor the timing is established by the
sources above.

The [Rellekka Wiki page](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Rellekka) also identifies the boat
east of the village as the route to the Iceberg. Before the multi-loc changes, its Travel option
uses the existing Iceberg landing. No normal-world recording of this particular option was found.
