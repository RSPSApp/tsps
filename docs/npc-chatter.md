# NPC chatter

NPCs that say overhead lines on their own: ducks ("Quack!"), sheep ("Baa!"), cows, the Grand
Exchange experts, the town criers, the Varrock newspaper seller, the Theatre of Blood orators,
the Goblin Village soldiers ("Red armour best!", "Stupid greenie!") and about 180 more.
`server/plugins/npcs/NpcChatter.plugin.js` plays them; the lines and their timing come from
rsprox captures.

## Live (rsprox captures)

Every `[say]` an NPC sends in the [rsprox.net](https://rsprox.net/database) transcripts was
collected, about 1,200 NPCs in all, then narrowed to the lines an NPC says unprompted:

- **Spacing is per NPC.** It is measured between two lines of one NPC that stayed in view in
  between. Duck median 89 ticks (5th-95th percentile 54-287); unsheared sheep 35 (25-48); cow 83
  (71-233); GE experts about 295 (208-390); town criers about 39 (21-57); ToB orators 7-8,
  almost fixed.
- **Some NPCs keep a cycle.** The five GE experts say their five lines in a fixed order.
- **Lines come with an animation or a sound.** A duck's quack plays area sound 413 (range 5),
  a sheep's 2053, a cow's or calf's 3044 (range 10) with animation 5854. The animation and sound
  together are the line's gesture, copied when it comes with 80% of the line's sightings.
- **Some NPCs alternate gestures.** A town crier rings his bell (animation 6865) on about 60% of
  his lines and scratches his head (6863, with sound 3816) on the rest. The Dorgesh-Kaan stall
  callers and the H.A.M. deacon also switch between gestures. These lines keep each gesture seen
  with at least 20% of them (and at least 5 times), weighted.
- **Fight chatter.** The Goblin Village red and green soldiers shout while they fight each other,
  every few ticks, with no player involved (recording 3271; local 1001339, 1001443-1001445,
  1001555). That is in-fighting, not idle chatter, so they speak only while fighting another NPC,
  and `npc-rivalries.json` now makes the two sides fight. The combat sound on a hit's tick
  (3517) is the hit's, not the line's.
- **Town criers rotate their news.** Draynor's crier said "Leagues V: Raging Echoes is here!" and
  "Christmas is here!" in rev 227, "Happy Halloween!" in rev 241, and "Hardcore Worlds are now
  live!" / "Week Two of Quest Month is here!" in rev 242. Only the current news is kept.

## Data

`server/data/definitions/npc-captured-chatter.json` is generated by
`python3 rsprox_index.py npc-says --tsps <tsps checkout> --out npc-captured-chatter.json` in
osrs-captures (`rsprox_npc_says.py`), after `link-cache`. Never edit it by hand; regenerate it.
Each NPC has its `lines` (text, sightings, and optional `seq` and `sound` or weighted `gestures`), `gap` (quantiles 5, 25,
50, 75 and 95, in ticks), `order` when it keeps a cycle, and `fighting` for fight chatter.

The extractor leaves out:

- lines said while the recording player targeted, talked to or fought that NPC;
- lines that come within 3 ticks of a player action in most of their sightings (answers to it);
- lines the generated Wiki transcripts (`npc-dialogue-index.json`, `npc-dialogues.json`) give only
  under a trigger: stealing, being attacked, a cutscene, "when ...", "upon ...", "during ...".
  A variant named for chance or timing ("at random", "every 50 ticks", "intermittent", goblin
  "in-fighting") keeps its lines, as does a line the Wiki doesn't have;
- lines seen in fewer than 2 recordings (this also drops any line naming a player), and NPCs seen
  in fewer than 3 recordings or with fewer than 5 measured gaps;
- random event, Sailing crew and quest-instance NPCs (`macro_*`, `sailing_crew_*`, `qip_*`, the
  random event invitations);
- retired lines. The NPC's newest revision with 3 recordings of it is "now", and lines weigh their
  sightings there. A line missing from it is dropped when its older share says it should have
  come 5 or more times, or when "now" already has 200 or more of the NPC's lines (a crier's past
  news). A line too rare to expect stays;
- blank lines, and the gesture of a line said in a fight (a hit or health bar on the NPC within
  10 ticks): those animations and sounds are the fight's attacks, blocks and combat sounds.

Gestures, like weights, come from the current revision when it has the line. Rev 242 transcripts
name their sounds (`id=sheep_atmospheric1 (2053)`), which the extractor reads alongside the bare
ids of older revisions.

`server/plugins/npcs/data/npc-chatter.json` is hand-written. `skipNpcs` lists NPCs whose lines
belong to code that drives them or that only answer a player: bosses, minigames, cutscenes, the
player-owned house and events, plus NPCs other plugins already make speak. Those are the Viking
shouters, Sailing's current ducks, the Warriors' Guild's Shanomi and Kamfreena, the Keldagrim
ticket vendors, the Mort Myre ghasts (Nature Spirit's attack shouts), and the Varlamore wealthy
citizens, urchins and house owners (Thieving). The Blast Furnace foreman is a minigame NPC: his
calls for repairs and fuel follow the machinery's state (`Machinery.BlastFurnace.js`).

`skipLines` lists text patterns, grouped by reason:
- lines answering another player's theft or pickpocket ("What do you think you're doing?",
  "Hey! Get your hands off there!"). No recording of your own actions can tell those apart from
  chatter, because the other player's click isn't in it;
- town crier banners recorded before the update they announce went live ("Hardcore Worlds open
  November 4th!"). The same revision has the "now live" line.

## Ours

- Only NPCs in an active region (near a real player, not a bot) are scheduled. Every 8 ticks the
  server's active NPCs are scanned for chatter NPCs, and each keeps its own next tick. A newly
  seen NPC's first line comes at a random point within one gap, so a field of sheep doesn't bleat
  in unison.
- Gaps are drawn by interpolating between the stored quantiles. Lines are drawn by weight, or in
  cycle order from a random start. A line with `gestures` draws one by weight each time.
- An NPC talking to or fighting a player, or dying, waits 5 ticks and tries again.

## Limitations

- **Measured gaps run long.** A gap only counts while the NPC stays in view, so NPCs that wander
  off screen leave long gaps out and short ones in. Medians are more reliable than the
  95th percentile.
- **News within a revision isn't dated.** Pre-launch banners like "Hardcore Worlds open
  November 4th!" are left out by `skipLines`; after the next regeneration, check the criers'
  lines for new ones.
- **Quest state is ignored.** The orators' lines before and after A Taste of Hope, for example,
  are both played.
- **Triggered lines aren't played yet.** Lines live sends on spawning, on being attacked or when
  a player approaches are left for a follow-up; the Wiki transcripts' variant names are the
  starting point.
