# Wizards' Tower bookcases

The library bookcases on the ground floor and in Traiborn's room contain eight random magical
book titles, listed on the Wiki's [Wizards' Tower](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Wizards%27_Tower)
and [Wizard Traiborn](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Wizard_Traiborn) pages, respectively.
Searching displays an introduction, the chosen title in quotes, and “Interesting...” in three
successive message boxes. Rsprox captures: recording 3202 (revision 232), ticks 861–1393.
The wall bookcase at (3104, 3158, 0) gives the same dialogue (ticks 1387–1393).

The ground-floor Wiki list differs from recording 3202's ground-floor titles in its spelling and
capitalisation of the titles about impressing mates, the elements, the wizard's husband, and the
mage. The Wiki's Traiborn list differs from the recorded husband title in capitalisation. The two
Wiki lists are used for their respective floors.
Ours: titles are equally likely; their exact selection weights are not established by the capture.
The basement clue bookcase and other Bookcase objects remain with their existing handlers.
